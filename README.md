## 🎮 About the Game

METAL GEAR SOLID 4: Guns of the Patriots is a stealth action game developed by Kojima Productions and published by Konami. The original PlayStation 3 release launched on June 12, 2008. The Master Collection Version was released on August 27, 2026, bringing Solid Snake's final mission to modern platforms.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **METAL GEAR SOLID 4: Guns of the Patriots - Master Collection Version**. This trainer provides optional single-player enhancements for stealth, combat, equipment, resources, and exploration. It is built around the game's real systems, including OctoCamo, the Psyche meter, CQC, weapon customization, Drebin Points, the Solid Eye, and Metal Gear Mk. II.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to replay the campaign with reduced resource management, experiment with weapons and equipment, practice stealth routes, explore mission areas, or revisit the story with flexible difficulty settings.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents Snake's life gauge from being depleted during normal gameplay.
- **Infinite Psyche** — Keeps Snake's Psyche meter full, reducing the effects of stress and battlefield pressure.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Infinite Ammo** — Provides unlimited ammunition for compatible firearms and weapons.
- **No Reload** — Removes the need to perform standard reloads while using supported weapons.
- **Rapid Fire** — Improves the firing rate of compatible weapons without changing their intended weapon class.

### 📌 Stealth

- **Stealth Mode** — Reduces or disables enemy detection, making it easier to use cover, camouflage, and silent takedowns.
- **No Alert** — Prevents standard enemy alert phases from being triggered.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Infinite Items** — Keeps supported rations, recovery items, ammunition types, and consumables available for repeated use.

### 📌 Resources

- **Infinite Drebin Points** — Provides a large or unlimited supply of Drebin Points for purchasing weapons, ammunition, and upgrades.

### 📌 Equipment

- **Infinite Battery** — Keeps the Solid Eye and Metal Gear Mk. II batteries charged while their supported functions are active.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use standard movement, crouching, crawling, cover, rolling, and contextual traversal as normal. Stealth Mode and No Alert are most useful when testing alternate routes, studying patrol patterns, or practicing movement through hostile areas. Keep trainer options disabled during scripted movement sequences if the game behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

Use Infinite Items to preserve rations, recovery supplies, ammunition, and other supported consumables. Infinite Drebin Points can be used at Drebin's shops to purchase weapons, ammunition, and customization options. The trainer does not replace the game's weapon selection or item management interfaces.

### World & Environment

OctoCamo changes Snake's camouflage appearance according to nearby surfaces, while the Solid Eye assists with visibility and battlefield information. Infinite Battery is useful when repeatedly using the Solid Eye or Metal Gear Mk. II during exploration. Save before entering major story transitions or loading a new act.

### Combat & Weapons

Infinite Health and Infinite Psyche improve survivability, while Infinite Ammo and No Reload simplify sustained firefights. CQC, close-quarters takedowns, weapon customization, suppressors, cover, and tranquilizer weapons remain part of the normal combat system. Disable aggressive combat options when practicing the game's intended stealth mechanics.
